# Attack on Titan team draft — local review

Seven rounds in the approved order: Regiment, Captain, Strategy teammate, Attack teammate, Defence teammate, Attack Titan shifter, Defence Titan shifter. The 35 choices are unique and keep the user-approved lot and mystery order. Full anime spoilers.

The existing auction and mystery rules remain: €2,500 per player, known starting prices 70/50/20, mystery prices 30/50/80. The narrator chooses one of two hidden answers per round and locks all seven before starting. Three shared clues fit either answer; each answer has three matching explanations. The ending asks players to debate which team wins and vote for another team. No damage or turn-based combat engine.

Shifter rounds pair the character with only the named Titan power. Armin remains the approved strategy teammate. Ymir means the 104th cadet. The order is a game-design choice for these roles, not an objective canon ranking.
